# Trust & Safety Model

Linghang is designed around a simple principle: **professional usefulness should not require silently upgrading uncertain information into legal truth.**

## Epistemic States Stay Distinct

The system preserves distinctions among:

- user allegation;
- source text;
- extracted information;
- model inference;
- candidate fact;
- verified/adjudicated fact;
- evidence;
- legal authority;
- authority application;
- recommendation;
- human decision / approval;
- planned action;
- completed action.

## Evidence Is Proposition-Bound

A valid source link is not enough. The relevant question is whether an exact passage supports the exact proposition being used.

The intended trust chain is:

```text
Source identity
→ Passage identity
→ semantic support relationship
→ proposition-specific validation
→ qualified/reliance-ready output
```

## Documents Are Not Instructions

Uploaded contracts, pleadings, emails, web pages, OCR text and tool results are treated as data. Embedded instructions inside those materials do not outrank the legal system's governing runtime instructions.

## Matter-Scoped Authorization

Global identity is not Matter authorization. A user's effective access is bounded by the grants for the current Matter, with separate document-level and sanitized-only restrictions where applicable.

## Proposed vs Approved

AI can propose a redline, action or decision. It does not become an approved change, sent filing, completed notice or other irreversible state merely because the model says so.

## Delivery vs Reliance

A document can be generated or delivered while the underlying legal proposition remains qualified. The UI can therefore distinguish **delivery complete** from **legal reliance ready**.

## Related Assets

Related images/files returned during research remain candidates. They do not become Facts, Evidence, Authority or verified proposition support until they enter the canonical Source/Evidence workflow.
